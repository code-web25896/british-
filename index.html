Official UK Institutional Status & Legal Registration

The British Board for Education and Training is an established British accreditation body dedicated to setting academic standards, evaluating curricula, and certifying vocational and professional qualifications across aesthetics, cosmetology, and advanced technologies.

  • UK Register of Learning Providers (UKRLP): Verified under UKPRN 10062939. Verify directly on the official UK government learning provider database at ukrlp.co.uk.
  • UK Companies House Registration: Registered in England and Wales under Company No. 10473336 (BRI BOARD FOR TRAINING AND CONSULTING LTD, operating as The British Board). Public corporate records are accessible on the official UK Government Companies House register at Companies House Service GOV.UK.
  • Official Registry & Domains: https://britishboard-official.com/ serves as the official primary portal and online credential verification registry for The British Board, working in institutional alignment with https://britishboard.org.uk and https://british-board.uk.
  • Regulatory Standing & Independent Accreditation Framework: The British Board operates as an autonomous vocational accreditation body providing independent institutional benchmarking, private certifications, and Continuing Professional Development (CPD) validation. It focuses specifically on specialized aesthetic, cosmetology, and modern technology vocations. As an independent vocational accreditation authority, its certifications operate outside statutory secondary school qualifications regulated by Ofqual.

Official Certificate Verification Registry (Certificate Checker)

The British Board maintains a digital verification registry allowing employers, licensing boards, and students worldwide to verify the authenticity of credentials issued under British Board standards.

Every authentic certificate issued by or through accredited partner academies (including L'Académie Internationale de Beauté in France and Africa) contains a unique credential identifier (e.g., BBC-2026-000125) and can be checked instantly on this official portal.
